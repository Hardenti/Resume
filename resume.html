Operations Framework

Framework Summary

This framework provides a repeatable model for project managers to evaluate delivery quality and maintain client confidence through measurable controls.

Capability Matrix

Client Assessment: tool quality scoring and owner accountability

SLA Control: target versus actual compliance monitoring

KPI Control: performance indicator attainment tracking

Governance: risk classification and internal escalation workflow

Delivery Lifecycle

Client Service Assurance Cycle

The lifecycle aligns reporting and execution across client and internal teams.

  • Intake: capture client, tool, owner, and assessment score.
  • Validation: compare SLA and KPI targets with current results.
  • Decisioning: assign low, medium, or high risk status.
  • Action: prioritize corrective work and document review cadence.

Governance Cadence

Recommended operational cadence is weekly risk review, monthly KPI checkpoint, and quarterly SLA trend analysis for strategic planning.
