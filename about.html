About ProjectOps Hub

ProjectOps Hub is a project-management website focused on operational control. It helps teams track client-assessed tools, confirm SLA achievement, and measure KPI performance while maintaining internal visibility on delivery risk.

The platform is intentionally simple to use: enter records consistently, review real-time status, and prioritize high-risk client accounts before service quality declines.

Operating Principles

  • Transparency: target versus actual performance is visible at the record level.
  • Accountability: each client-tool entry is owned and reviewed on a defined date.
  • Prevention: risk indicators help teams act before SLA or KPI misses become trends.

Workflow

1) Log client tool assessments. 2) Compare SLA and KPI targets with actual outcomes. 3) Review risk level and internal status. 4) Escalate and track corrective actions.
